Roles

Giovanni Roma is a computer music designer, sound designer and sound engineer. A doctoral researcher at the Conservatorio «Domenico Cimarosa» in Avellino, he studies computational strategies for the preservation of electroacoustic music, and works on live electronics, sound installations and electronic instrument building. Alongside his own practice, he has worked since 2001 as a post-production and mastering engineer for third parties, on material of every genre and for national and international labels.

Research

Aural-IA

My doctoral project investigates computational strategies for the preservation of electroacoustic music: works whose technological apparatus ages faster than the score that documents them, and which therefore call for methods of reconstruction, documentation and transmission suited to their nature.

Case studies draw on works by Harvey, Stockhausen and Boulez; the methods combine supervised geometric annotation of scores, human-in-the-loop workflows and retrieval-augmented generation.
